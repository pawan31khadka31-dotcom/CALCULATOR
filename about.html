About Hisab Calculator

A simple daily money and measurement tool for Nepal

Hisab Calculator is a free, easy-to-use web app that helps people in Nepal do the everyday calculations that matter: loans, savings, expenses and conversions. It is made to be clear and quick for everyone, including people who are not comfortable with complicated finance tools, and it works in both English and Nepali.

What you can do

  • Saikada Byaj: calculate interest the Nepali way (Rs per 100 per month), by duration or between two BS dates.
  • SIP and EMI: estimate investment growth and loan instalments, with an optional month-by-month table.
  • Income & Expense: record daily Amdani and Kharcha and see them as a table and a bar chart.
  • Calculators: basic and scientific calculator with percentage.
  • Converters: BS ↔ AD dates, units (including ropani, aana, bigha, tola and lal) and currencies.
  • Notes, reminders, gold and silver price calculation and a formula reference.

Why people use it

  • Works offline: install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on your phone and use it without internet after the first visit. Live currency rates need an internet connection.
  • Fast and lightweight: a small download that opens quickly, even on slow connections and older phones.
  • Private by design: your calculations and notes stay on your own device. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Free to use: no sign-up is needed for the calculators.

Who made it

Hisab Calculator was created by Pawan Khadka to make everyday hisab (accounting) simpler for Nepalese users. Feedback from users guides every new tool and improvement.
