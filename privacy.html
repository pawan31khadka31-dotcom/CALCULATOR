Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hisab Calculator (“we”, “us”, “the app”), created by Pawan Khadka and available at https://pawan31khadka31-dotcom.github.io/CALCULATOR/, handles information when you use our calculators and tools. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we do not collect

You do not need to create an account or give us your name, phone number or email address to use the calculators. The calculations you run (Saikada Byaj, SIP, EMI, unit, currency and date conversions) are done inside your own browser. We do not run a server that receives or stores your calculations or the numbers you type.

2. Data stored on your device (local storage)

To work smoothly and offline, the app saves some information only on your own device, in your browser’s local storage. This can include calculation history (where the app saves it), income and expense entries, notes, reminders, your language and light/dark choice, and downloaded currency rates. This information is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time with the “Clear History” or “Delete” buttons in the app, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, this saved information may be lost.

3. Services the app connects to

When you use the app, your browser may contact the following services. These requests do not contain the numbers or notes you enter, but the service can see your IP address and browser details, as happens on any website.

  • Hosting: the site is served by GitHub Pages and may be delivered through Cloudflare. These providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and time of visit).
  • Google Fonts: to display text in the intended typefaces.
  • Currency rate providers: the Currency Converter downloads public exchange rates from third-party rate services.
  • Excel export library: if you choose “Export to Excel”, a spreadsheet library is loaded from a public content delivery network.
  • Optional cloud sync (only if turned on): if the site owner enables Google sign-in, signing in will share your Google account name and email with Google Firebase, and the data you choose to sync is stored in a Firebase database linked to your account. You can sign out at any time, and you can ask us to delete your synced data using the contact details below. Cloud sync is not used unless you sign in.

  • Google may use the DoubleClick (DART) cookie to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google’s advertising technologies.
  • Where the law requires it (for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before ads that use cookies or personal data are shown.

5. Cookies

The app itself does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Cookies may be set by third parties, such as Google when ads are shown. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some ads or features may then work differently.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose calculator and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

The site is delivered over HTTPS, and your calculations stay on your device. No method of storing or transmitting data is completely secure, so please do not store highly sensitive information such as bank passwords in notes.

8. Your choices

  • Delete saved data in the app or in your browser settings.
  • Opt out of personalised ads using the links in section 4.
  • Contact us to ask about, or request deletion of, any data linked to your account (if you used optional sign-in).

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date above shows when it last changed. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
